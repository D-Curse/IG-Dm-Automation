Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 October 2026 · Last updated: 10 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the operator of DM-Automation (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information when providing Instagram reel analytics and comment-triggered messaging automation. For privacy questions or requests, contact dhruvbhatada@gmail.com.

1. Information we process

  • Connected account information: the Instagram professional account identifier and access token supplied by an authorized account operator. Tokens enable access to Meta APIs within the permissions granted. We do not request Instagram passwords.
  • Reel information: media identifiers, captions, publication timestamps, and Instagram reel links.
  • Comment information: comment identifiers, text, usernames, author identifiers where available, timestamps, and replies. We read these to detect keyword matches, avoid replying to the connected account, and check whether a configured reply already exists.
  • Automation settings and history: configured keywords, public reply text, outgoing private message templates, enabled rules, triggering comment text and username, comment and reel identifiers, send or skip status, error details, and processing timestamps.
  • Analytics: aggregate reel metrics made available by Instagram, such as views, reach, likes, comments, saves, and shares. These are temporarily cached in server memory.
  • Operational information: session and security information, service events, and diagnostic logs. Hosting providers, if used, may process IP addresses and request metadata for delivering and securing the service.
  • Support communications: information you choose to include when emailing us, including information needed to verify a privacy request.

The current application sends configured private replies; it does not provide a feature for retrieving your full inbox or storing incoming private-message conversations.

2. How we use information

We use information to discover reels on the connected account, apply rules selected by the account operator, send permitted comment replies and private replies, display analytics, prevent duplicate sends, diagnose failures, secure the service, respond to requests, and meet applicable legal and platform obligations. Comment-triggered messages are automated and use text configured by the account operator.

3. Permissions and your choices

Access depends on the permissions granted to the Meta app. The service uses account/media access, comment management, messaging, and insights access as needed for the enabled features. You may disable a reel rule, pause background processing, or stop the server. An account operator can revoke app access using Instagram or Meta's connected-app settings. Revoking access stops future API access but does not automatically erase information already stored by this application; request deletion as described below.

4. Sharing and service providers

We send the relevant account, comment, and message information to Meta/Instagram to perform requested API actions. Authorized account operators can view automation history in their dashboard. Hosting, infrastructure, or email providers may process information to deliver their services if used. We may disclose information when legally required or reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, and service security. We do not sell personal information, use comment data for advertising profiles, or share it with unrelated advertisers. Instagram separately handles information under its own privacy policies.

5. Storage, retention, and security

The current application stores reel settings and automation records in a SQLite database on the operator's server or computer. Access tokens are held in server configuration and process memory. Comment and action history remains stored until the operator removes it or fulfills a deletion request; this version does not automatically expire those records. Duplicate-protection records help prevent repeat messages. Recent service events are limited to the latest 100 database entries. Analytics responses are cached for approximately 60 seconds; expired cache entries may remain in memory until overwritten or the process restarts. Logs and any operator-created backups can also contain operational records.

The application uses server-side tokens and session security controls. The current dashboard is intended for private localhost access; the dashboard now requires an administrator login, uses password hashing and session expiry, and limits login attempts. HTTPS and deployment access controls are still required before making the dashboard publicly accessible. No storage or transmission system can be guaranteed completely secure. Do not send passwords or access tokens in support emails.

6. Deletion and other privacy requests

To request access, correction, deletion, or to object to processing where applicable, email dhruvbhatada@gmail.com. Identify the relevant Instagram username and, if helpful, a reel or comment link. We may request proportionate information to verify the request. We will respond without undue delay and within applicable legal deadlines. We will delete information that is no longer needed or that we are required to delete, subject to limited legal retention obligations. See our Data Deletion Instructions.

7. Cookies, external links, and transfers

The dashboard uses an essential session cookie for session integrity and protection against forged requests. The application does not intentionally use advertising cookies. Dashboard fonts may be loaded from Google Fonts, which can receive network request metadata. Links to Instagram and other third-party services are governed by those services' policies. Meta and any selected service providers may process information in countries other than your own; applicable safeguards must be used where required.

8. Children

DM-Automation is intended for operators of professional Instagram accounts and is not directed to children. Do not use it to knowingly collect children's personal information in violation of applicable law. Contact us if you believe such information has been processed so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

9. Updates and contact

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the service or applicable requirements. We will update the date above and provide additional notice where required. Privacy contact: dhruvbhatada@gmail.com.
