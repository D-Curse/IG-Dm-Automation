Connect your Instagram

Use a professional account and the official Instagram API with Instagram Login.

Set up live mode

  1. Create a Meta developer app with Instagram API access and authorize your Business or Creator account.
  2. Request instagram_business_basic, instagram_business_manage_comments, and instagram_business_manage_messages. Access for other accounts may require Meta review.
  3. Copy .env.example to .env and set your Instagram account ID, access token, and a long random secret key.
  4. Restart the server, sync your reels, and configure each rule. Keep this dashboard private on your computer.

Tokens stay on the server. Demo rules and live rules use separate reel IDs. New reels are always disabled until you configure them.
